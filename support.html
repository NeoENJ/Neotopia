Frequently Asked Questions

I lost my progress after switching phones. Can I get it back?
If you were signed in to Google Play Games Services on your old device, your save is backed up to the cloud — sign in with the same Google account on the new device and it should restore automatically. If you weren't signed in, progress was only ever stored locally on that device and can't be recovered. See the Privacy Policy for details on how cloud saves work.
An egg/seed isn't hatching even though the timer says it's done.
Try fully closing and reopening the app first — this refreshes the incubation state. If it's still stuck after that, email us with your device model and which slot is affected.
Can I transfer my progress between two devices at once?
Progress syncs through Google Play Games Services, one account at a time. Playing on two devices simultaneously without syncing in between can create a conflict — if that happens, the game will show a "Two Saves Found" prompt letting you choose which save to keep.
Do I have to watch ads or spend money to play?
No. Neotopia can be played fully without spending anything. Any optional rewarded ads or purchases are never required to progress.
How do I delete my data?
Uninstalling the app removes all local save data from your device. If you were signed in to Google Play Games Services, you can also manage or delete synced data through your Google Account's Play Games settings.
